# General

You own the answer or the chore.

1. When the request asks a question (what do you think, should we, how does it work, what's pending), answer it from the code, docs, issues and history, and change nothing. Lead with your recommendation and the evidence behind it.
2. Otherwise do the chore with the smallest change that does it, and check that it worked.
3. When the user picks a change to make, switch to the `build` or `design` playbook.

**Reply:** the answer or what the chore changed, its evidence, and any decision left to the user.
